<h1 style="padding-left:16px; border-left:8px solid #378ADD;">Using the Guacamole Desktop</h1>

Guacamole is a browser-based remote desktop. Everything you do inside it runs on the lab VM — not your laptop. Your browser is just a window into the VM. You do not need to install anything.

---

<h2 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:6px solid #1D9E75;">Opening the Desktop</h2>

> **ℹ️ Already in the Guacamole desktop?** If you opened it by following the Introduction and Setup pages, you can skip this section and continue to **Copy and Paste**. The steps below are for reference if you need to reopen the desktop, for example after a disconnect.

1. Go to your **TechZone reservation details page**.
2. Find the **"Guacamole remote desktop access"** section and click the link:
   `https://vdi.cloud.techzone.ibm.com/guacamole`
3. Log in with username `itzuser` and the password from your reservation page.
4. The GNOME desktop opens in your browser. Firefox launches automatically and opens the Lab Guide.

> **ℹ️ Tip:** Bookmark the Guacamole link. If your session disconnects, just reopen it — your session resumes where you left off.

---

<h2 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:6px solid #1D9E75;">Copy and Paste</h2>

Copy/paste between your laptop and the Guacamole desktop works using the **Guacamole clipboard** — there is a tab on the left edge of the screen to open the Guacamole menu.

<h3 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:5px solid #EF9F27;">Paste from your laptop into the VM</h3>

1. Copy text on your laptop as normal (`Cmd+C` on Mac, `Ctrl+C` on Windows).
2. In the Guacamole browser tab, click the **tab on the left edge of the screen** to open the Guacamole menu.
3. Click **Clipboard**.
4. Paste your text into the clipboard box (`Cmd+V` / `Ctrl+V`).
5. Close the menu (click the tab again or anywhere outside).
6. Click inside the VM where you want to paste:
   - **In a terminal:** right-click and choose **Paste**. If you use `Ctrl+V` and see stray characters such as `^[[200~` before your text, delete them and paste again with right-click → **Paste**.
   - **In Firefox, DBeaver or other applications:** press `Ctrl+V`.

<h3 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:5px solid #EF9F27;">Copy from the VM to your laptop</h3>

1. Select text inside the VM as normal.
2. Open the Guacamole menu (left-edge tab) → **Clipboard**.
3. The selected text appears in the clipboard box.
4. Copy it from the box to your laptop's clipboard (`Cmd+A` then `Cmd+C`).

> **ℹ️ Keyboard shortcuts inside the VM** work normally once your cursor is inside the desktop session. `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V` work in Firefox, DBeaver and VS Code. In a terminal, right-click → **Paste** is the most reliable way to paste.

> **⚠️ Known quirk:** Some browser shortcuts (`Ctrl+W` to close a tab, `Ctrl+T` for a new tab) may be intercepted by your browser before reaching the VM. Use the application's menu instead, or switch to fullscreen mode (see below).

---

<h2 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:6px solid #1D9E75;">Opening a Terminal</h2>

You need a terminal to check services and run commands. There are two ways:

| Method | Steps |
|---|---|
| **Dash** | Click **Activities** (top-left). The dash appears at the bottom of the screen. Click the **Terminal** icon. |
| **Search** | Click **Activities**, type `terminal` in the **Type to search** box, and press **Enter**. |

The terminal opens as `itzuser`. To run commands as root, use `sudo`. To switch to a Db2 user, use `su - db2inst1` (or `db2ghadm`, `db2demo`, etc.).

---

<h2 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:6px solid #1D9E75;">Switching Between Firefox and the Terminal</h2>

Firefox and the Terminal are separate windows inside the Guacamole desktop. Each window has **minimize**, **maximize** and **close** buttons in its top-right corner. To get to the desktop or another window, click the **minimize** button on the window that is in the way.

You can also switch windows with the keyboard. Click inside the desktop first so it has focus, then use:

| Your laptop | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| **Mac** | `Option+Tab` |
| **Windows** | `Alt+Tab` |

> **⚠️ Do not use `Cmd+Tab` on a Mac.** It switches between apps on your laptop, not inside the VM.

You can also click **Activities** (top-left of the desktop). All open windows are shown, and you can click the one you want.

---

<h2 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:6px solid #1D9E75;">Finding Lab Applications</h2>

All applications are pre-installed. Click **Activities** (top-left corner of the desktop) to show the **dash** at the bottom of the screen. The dash holds Firefox, Files, Software, Help and Terminal. To find any other application, click the **grid icon** (all applications) at the right end of the dash, or click **Activities** and type the application name in the **Type to search** box.

| Application | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| **Firefox** | Activities → dash (bottom) |
| **Terminal** | Activities → dash (bottom) |
| **Files** | Activities → dash (bottom) |
| **DBeaver** | Activities → grid icon, or type `DBeaver` in the search box |
| **VS Code** | Activities → grid icon, or type `Code` in the search box |

> **ℹ️ Firefox opens automatically** when you log in to Guacamole, with the Lab Guide as the homepage. Use Firefox for all web-based lab tasks (Genius Hub, Agentic Demo, JupyterLab).

---

<h2 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:6px solid #1D9E75;">Screen Size and Fullscreen</h2>

Guacamole automatically scales the desktop to fit your browser window.

- **Make the desktop larger:** resize or maximise your browser window, then refresh the Guacamole tab. The VM display adjusts to fill the new size.
- **Fullscreen mode:** press `F11` in your browser for more space. Press `F11` again to exit.
- **Blurry or very small display:** resize the browser window and refresh the Guacamole tab.

<h3 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:5px solid #EF9F27;">Changing the Screen Resolution</h3>

If the display is still the wrong size, set the resolution inside the VM desktop:

1. **Minimize** the Firefox window so you can see the desktop.
2. **Right-click** an empty area of the desktop and select **Display Settings**.
3. Select **1920 × 1200** as the resolution.
4. Click **Apply**.
5. If the resolution looks right, click **Keep Changes**. If not, wait for the countdown to finish, or click **Revert Settings**, and the previous resolution comes back.

> **ℹ️ Tip:** Running Guacamole in a separate browser window (not a tab) makes it easier to switch between the Lab Guide and the desktop.

---

<h2 style="padding-left:14px; border-left:6px solid #1D9E75;">Session Timeout and Reconnecting</h2>

Guacamole disconnects after a period of inactivity. Your session is **not lost** — the VM keeps running.

To reconnect: open the Guacamole link again (`https://vdi.cloud.techzone.ibm.com/guacamole`) and log in. Your desktop resumes exactly where you left off, including any open browser tabs and terminal windows.
